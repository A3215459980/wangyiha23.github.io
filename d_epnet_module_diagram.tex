\caption{Module diagram of the proposed D-EPNet detector. The left panel shows the outer iterative process from graph preprocessing to the final hard decision. The right panel details the information flow within one iteration: EP computes cavity statistics, DIFFormer performs graph-aware interaction modeling, the GRU updates recurrent states, and the readout network produces symbol logits that are used to refine the EP site parameters for the next iteration.}
